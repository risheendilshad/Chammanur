Behind this site

One village, one dream

From Chammanur, for Chammanur

This website is an idea of two people born and raised in Chammanur.

Work and life have carried many of us far from home, yet Chammanur stays with each of us. We spoke about how much it would mean to have a place of our own, where the village's thoughts, ideas, stories and everyday news could be shared.

That idea became the site you are reading today. It belongs not to one or two people, but to everyone who loves Chammanur.

A place where those who stayed and those who left can gather together.
